# Текст озвучки

Stare at the red dot. Now snap your eyes to the clock. Did that first tick feel longer?

You're not imagining it. Scientists call it chronostasis. That literally means time standing still.

Your eyes jump several times every second. During each jump, your vision mostly shuts off. You never notice the blackout.

So your brain fills the gap. It stretches the new image backward in time. That's why the first second drags.

It happens every time you look around. You only catch it with a clock. Be honest: did yours freeze?
