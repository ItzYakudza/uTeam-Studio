TITLE
Why Your Brain Can't Find a Word (Tip-of-the-Tongue)

DESCRIPTION
You know the word. You can almost say it. But it won't come out. This is the tip-of-the-tongue state: a temporary word-retrieval failure.

SOURCES:
- Brown, R. & McNeill, D. (1966). The “tip of the tongue” phenomenon. Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior, 5(4), 325–337. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-5371(66)80040-3
- Schwartz, B. L. & Metcalfe, J. (2011). Tip-of-the-tongue (TOT) states: retrieval, behavior, and experience. Memory & Cognition, 39, 737–749. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-010-0066-8
