# Текст озвучки

The second you lie down, your brain starts a debate. It replays awkward moments and invents tomorrow's worries. Why?

By day, noise and screens keep you busy. At night it goes quiet, and the leftovers surface. Worries come first.

Stress keeps your brain on alert. Researchers call it pre-sleep arousal. Poor sleepers show more of it.

Trying to stop them backfires. Told not to think of a white bear, people kept thinking of it. Pushing thoughts away keeps them close.

So try this. Write tomorrow's to-do list for five minutes. In a sleep lab study, that beat writing about finished tasks by about nine minutes.

Empty your head onto paper tonight. Give your thoughts somewhere to go. Or the debate starts again.
