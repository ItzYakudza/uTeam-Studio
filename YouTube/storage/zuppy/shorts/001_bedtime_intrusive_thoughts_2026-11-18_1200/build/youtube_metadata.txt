TITLE
Why Your Brain Overthinks Right Before Sleep #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Why do intrusive thoughts show up the moment you lie down? Quiet, stress, and trying to push thoughts away can all keep your mind racing. A five-minute to-do list may help.
