Don't you dare yawn right now. I bet you will anyway. Here is why it happens.

A yawn is weirdly contagious. Seeing one can trigger yours. Even reading about it works.

About half of adults catch them. Toddlers usually do not catch them. That changes around age four or five.

Dogs can catch human yawns too. Most dogs did in one study. Family and friends catch yawns most.

So why do we yawn at all? One idea says yawns cool the brain. Another links them to empathy and bonding.

Scientists still debate both ideas. Now be honest, did you yawn? Watch again and do not yawn.
