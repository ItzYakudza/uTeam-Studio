# Voiceover — Everyday Brain Glitches: The Doorway Effect

You enter a room—and forget why. It feels like a mental reset.

It's called the doorway effect. Some experiments found memory dips after a room change.

Your brain may be updating its model of the current event. That shift can interfere with a goal held in mind.

But later experiments found the effect isn't universal. Has a doorway ever erased your mission?
