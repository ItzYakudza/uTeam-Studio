TITLE
Why You Forget Why You Entered a Room

DESCRIPTION
You walk into a room and suddenly forget your mission. This is known as the doorway effect—but the science is more nuanced than the name suggests.

- McFadyen, J. et al. (2021). Doorways do not always cause forgetting: a multimodal investigation. BMC Psychology, 9, 41. doi:10.1186/s40359-021-00536-3
