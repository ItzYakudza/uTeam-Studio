TITLE
How Children Were Treated Centuries Ago (And Why So Many Didn't Survive)

DESCRIPTION
For most of history, close to half of all children did not reach their fifth birthday. This animated story shows how parents and doctors tried to treat children before germ theory, from swaddling and the teething panic to opium syrups, mercury powders, rickets, smallpox, dirty milk, and the discoveries that finally changed everything.
